### 1. Story Identity

- **ID**: US-003
- **Title**: Resolve equipment uncertainty
- **Priority**: Must
- **Persona**: HR coordinator

### 2. Narrative

As an HR coordinator, I want the product to verify alternatives so a possibility is not reported as readiness.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given “The laptop arrives Thursday; a loaner may be available Monday,” when that reply is applied, then an unconfirmed alternative is recorded and IT is prompted to confirm availability and ready date, citing P2.
2. Given an explicit authorized commitment that meets BR-4, when it is applied, then equipment is confirmed. Given “I’ll try,” when it is applied, then equipment is not confirmed.
3. Given a negative answer showing no device by the start date, when it is applied, then equipment is blocked and escalated. Given silence, when the request deadline passes, then equipment remains unconfirmed and escalates.
4. Given sufficient authorized evidence after escalation, when it arrives, then the factual escalation is cleared and the original messages and escalation remain visible.

### 4. Scope Notes

- **In Scope for MVP**: Jordan’s and Riley’s Appendix A equipment sequences; one IT follow-up per clarification round; assigned laptop or permitted loaner; other tasks continue while equipment is unresolved.
- **Deferred**: Asset-tag capture is not required. Production IT fulfillment and live messaging are out of this release.
